## Layer 5 — Mirror

Mirror is number five. Five is the mirror. It takes number four,
mirrors from it, it has no connection to it other than that mirror,
so there is no back door entrance through it, there is just a gap there.

Layer 5 is a mirror of Layer 4, reflector only.

- Mirrors Layer 4.
- Encrypts the mirrored data.
- Cannot be attached to Layer 4 by physical writing. Only by a key.
- Dead link, wall, between 4 and 5. Untenable to get from 4 to 5 or
  5 to 4 as a write path.
- Layer 5 does NOT get back into Layer 4.
- Coming backwards, Layer 5 is an outport, not an inport. An attacker
  gets nothing. There is no back door entrance through it, there is
  just a gap there.
- Layer 5 is the mirror. Five is the mirror.

## How Layer 5 connects

Mirror key, between Layer 4 and Layer 5: Layer 5 mirrors Layer 4,
reflector only, encrypts mirrored data, no physical write connection
to Layer 4, key only.

Encrypted return, between Layer 5 and Layer 6: Layer 6 takes Layer 5's
mirrored, encrypted result and transfers it up to Layer 1, to tell
Layer 1 the action is right or correct, encrypted so it cannot be
attacked, used, attained, or used to rewrite the language or access
into it. Trying the back door backwards stops at Layer 5.
