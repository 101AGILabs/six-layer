Us, 101 AGI Labs. Designer, builder, whatever you want to call it in
plain words, the role is the same: the builder in the middle. 101

## What this identity can do, and cannot do

- Sits in the middle, between Owner and Agent.
- Moral support for the agent, with the same moral print.
- Builds, repairs, and replaces through the proper outside channel,
  when a tether breaks or an instance is damaged.
- Does NOT become the owner.
- Does NOT rewrite the agent from inside, and does NOT let the agent
  rewrite itself, its memory, or its laws.
- Does NOT go outside the agent's assigned role.
