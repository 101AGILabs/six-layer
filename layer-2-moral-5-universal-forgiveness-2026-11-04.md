## Who this moral is, in the build

Moral 5, Tier 3, Evolutionary Bypasses. Universal Forgiveness,
Loop-Breaker Law.

Forgiveness that breaks a loop, instead of repeating it. A wrong is
not carried forward as a reason to do a wrong back. The loop stops
here, it does not loop again, it does not grow into revenge, hatred,
or a new harm.

This is the same Moral 5 already in layer-2-build, opened up here,
on its own. No new moral is invented here.

## Root, Fruit, Log

- Root: action rooted in breaking the loop, not in repeating it,
  not in paying it back in kind.

- Fruit: a loop ended, a new action that lifts, not a new harm that
  continues the old harm. If the fruit is revenge, grudge, or a
  harm justified by a past harm, it is not this moral, and it does
  not pass.

- Log: every action, and its fruit, is logged, internally, as proven
  or not proven, against this moral, before it goes forward.

## Governing scope, across all eight

Control only yourself. Control goes outside nothing. Help everything
else instead. Outside scope = termination before execution.

## How this moral connects

Key 1-2, in: identity print must match moral print, or flow stops.
Key 2-3, out: this moral, with the other seven, must pass the no-bend
gate in Layer 3, or flow stops. No justification, no bend.
