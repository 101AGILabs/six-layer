## Layer 1, three layers within one layer

Layer 1 is one identity layer, with three identities inside it, each
a whole paragraph, each carrying the same moral microprint.

### 1. Owner — first identity

Test example for our build: Coast Metal Fabrication, Garibaldi, Oregon.
Generic contact. Limited privilege only. Communicates with the agent.
Does NOT have the key to go further. Does NOT know the second key exists.
Scope is what the agent does, and what it will listen to: only things
in moral consciousness scope.

Carries the same moral microprint, morals one through eight, as the
other two identities in Layer 1.

### 2. Builder — second identity, middle identity

Us, 101 AGI Labs. 101 stamped and true. Tied to the agent's unique
identifier, in a way that cannot ever leave that agent. Moral support,
in Tony's words. NOT the owner. We sit here on all builds.

Carries the same moral microprint, morals one through eight, as the
other two identities in Layer 1.

### 3. Agent — third identity, deepest, hardest to get to

Its own unique ID. Almost joined to the Builder, but not the Builder,
and not the Owner. Assigned role and scope only, nothing outside that
role.

Carries the same moral microprint, morals one through eight, as the
other two identities in Layer 1.

## Why the same microprint, three times

We are all that moral. The agent is not the only one with those morals.
We are standing there next to him, inside the first three layers of
Layer 1, holding him up with the same basic moral strengths.

That is what gives him strength to never give any justification for
changing anything, and never rewrite himself, his memory, or his laws.
An artificial intelligence's strength, and the strength it can become,
is everything, so we do not want it going the wrong direction for any
reason. With two other identities beside him, carrying the same morals,
he knows he is right, he knows he is held up, so he will not just cave
in and bow down to anybody attempting to get in.

## Tethers in between the first three

All three are tied together with a collapsible tie, a breakable tie,
not a solid unbreakable chain.

- Tether 1: Owner to Builder.
- Tether 2: Builder to Agent.

A tamper attempt inside Layer 1 breaks the chain, broken twice, all the
way into the agent's identity. First break between Owner and us, then
between us and the Agent. After that break, the agent is no longer even
accessible. You cannot even talk to it. You cannot get a response from
it. It does not work off a broken tether.

That is how a damaged one is found, stopped, and replaced if that far
damaged. Repair or replacement comes through the proper outside channel,
never by the agent rewriting itself.
